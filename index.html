About

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, under the supervision of Dirk Becherer and Peter Friz. My position is jointly based at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin and Technische Universität Berlin and is funded through CRC/TRR 388 “Rough Analysis, Stochastic Dynamics & Related Fields” , Project A07. I am also an affiliated member of the IRTG 2544 “Stochastic Analysis in Interaction” .

My current research focuses on rough and singular dynamics, with particular emphasis on hybrid rough stochastic systems, backward SDEs, rough PDEs, and singular PDEs.
