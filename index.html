About

I am a PhD student in mathematics in Berlin. My research lies at the intersection of stochastic analysis, rough path theory, stochastic differential equations, and partial differential equations.

I am particularly interested in pathwise approaches to stochastic systems, equations with discontinuous or singular inputs, and probabilistic representations of nonlinear PDEs.

Research

Rough Paths

Rough differential equations, discontinuous drivers, decorated paths, and stability.

BSDEs & PDEs

Backward stochastic differential equations and their connections with nonlinear and singular PDEs.

Singular SPDEs

KPZ-type equations, paracontrolled methods, and probabilistic representations.
