About

I am a PhD student in Mathematics at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, under the supervision of Dirk Becherer and Peter Friz. I am also affiliated with Technische Universität Berlin. My research lies at the intersection of stochastic analysis, rough path theory, stochastic differential equations and partial differential equations.

I am particularly interested in pathwise approaches to stochastic systems, backward stochastic differential equations, singular PDEs and SPDEs, and probabilistic representations of nonlinear equations.
